Cashier User Manual
A complete, step-by-step guide to recording a student's unpaid balance from a previous school year, collecting payment against it, issuing an Official Receipt, and understanding how it appears on the Statement of Account.
I-SMS  ·  CASHIER MODULE

1

What is a Back Account?

A back account is money a student still owes from a previous school year. It is separate from this year's tuition and fees. This module lets the Cashier record that old debt against the student, collect it (in full or partially), and issue a receipt.

The one rule to remember A back account is a warning on the Statement of Account — it is never added to the SOA's total due. This year's bill stays this year's bill. The old debt is shown so the student knows to settle it, but it does not change the monthly amount they are being billed.

Why it is tied to the student, not the enrolment

The back account is linked to the student. That means it follows them from year to year. A debt from S.Y. 2023-2024 will keep appearing on their SOA in 2026-2027, and every year after, until it is paid or cancelled.

The four statuses

StatusWhat it meansShows on the SOA?
UnpaidRecorded, nothing paid yet.Yes — full amount
PartialSome payment received, a balance remains.Yes — remaining balance
PaidFully settled. Balance is zero.No — it disappears
CancelledWritten off or entered in error. Record kept for audit.No — it disappears
Status is automatic You never set the status by hand. The system moves it Unpaid → Partial → Paid as payments come in, and back again if a payment is voided.

Where the existing records came from

All 491 historical back-account records from S.Y. 2023-2024 were already imported into this module, including 351 unpaid accounts. They are searchable from day one — you do not need to re-enter them. You only record a back account manually for a debt the system does not already know about.

Task 1 — Record a Back Account

Use this when a student has an old unpaid balance that is not yet in the system.

1
Open the ➕ Record a Back Account panel.

It is open by default at the top of the page.

2
Find the student.

Type at least 2 characters of their surname or LRN. A list appears as you type. Use the ↑ / ↓ arrow keys to move through the results and press Enter to select — or just click the name.

3
Confirm the right student is picked.

Their name, LRN and last enrolled school year appear in a blue box. If it is the wrong person, click change and search again.

4
Enter the S.Y. of the debt.

The school year the money was owed from — for example 2023-2024. Not the current year. Pick from the dropdown or type it.

5
Enter the Amount.

The full amount owed, in pesos. Must be greater than zero.

6
Optionally add Grade & Section and Remarks.

Remarks are free text — use them to explain the debt, e.g. "unpaid tuition balance carried from S.Y. 2023-2024". This helps whoever reads the record later.

7
Click Save Back Account.

The record is created as Unpaid and you are taken straight to it, ready to collect.

Task 2 — Collect a Payment

This is the main daily task: taking money against an old balance and issuing the receipt.

1
Find the student in the list and click Collect.
2
Check the three boxes: Original, Paid, Balance.

Confirm the balance matches what the payer expects before taking money.

3
Enter the Amount to collect.

It defaults to the full remaining balance. If the student is paying only part of it, type the smaller amount — partial payments are allowed.

4
Choose the Method.

Cash, GCash, Maya, Bank, or Check.

5
Enter a Reference # if not cash.

Optional, but strongly recommended for GCash / Maya / Bank / Check so the payment can be traced later.

6
Click Collect & Print OR and confirm.

The receipt opens automatically and the print dialog appears. The balance updates immediately.

The receipt tells the student where they stand Every back-account OR shows a box at the bottom: either FULLY SETTLED in green, or the Remaining back-account balance in red. The student leaves the counter knowing exactly what is left.
It counts toward your day Back-account collections automatically join the day's totals, so they are included in your End-of-Day Close and the Collection Report. You do not record them anywhere else.

Task 3 — Reprint or Void a Payment

Every payment ever taken is listed under Payment History on the student's back-account screen.

Reprinting a receipt

1
Open the student's record (click Collect or View).
2
In Payment History, click Reprint on the payment you need.

The original receipt opens with the same OR number. Reprinting does not create a new receipt or a new number.

Voiding a payment

Void a payment only when it was recorded in error — wrong amount, wrong student, or a cancelled transaction.

1
In Payment History, click Void on that payment.
2
Confirm.

The payment is struck through and marked Voided. The student's balance is restored, the status rolls back (e.g. Paid → Partial), and the amount is removed from the day's collection totals.

A void is permanent and recorded A voided payment cannot be un-voided, and the voided receipt is stamped VOID when printed. Every void is written to the audit log with your name, the date, and the amount. If you voided by mistake, simply collect the payment again — a new OR number will be issued.
6

Task 4 — Correct or Cancel a Record

Editing an amount

Use Edit if the recorded amount, school year, section or remarks were wrong.

1
Click Edit on the row.
2
Change the values and click Save.

The balance and status are recalculated automatically from the payments already made.

You cannot set the amount lower than what was already paid If a student has paid ₱1,000, the amount cannot be edited below ₱1,000 — the system will refuse and tell you why. This protects the receipts you have already issued. Void the payments first if you truly need to go lower.

Cancelling a back account

Use Cancel when the debt should not be collected at all — it was entered by mistake, or management has written it off.

1
Click Cancel on the row and confirm.
2
The record becomes Cancelled.

Its balance drops to zero and it stops appearing on the student's SOA. The record itself is kept for audit — nothing is deleted.

Cancel is blocked once money has been received The Cancel option disappears as soon as a payment exists on the account. This is deliberate: you cannot erase a debt the school has already collected against. Void the payments first, then cancel.

Quick decision guide

SituationWhat to do
Wrong amount typed, no payment yetEdit the amount.
Wrong amount, payment already takenVoid the payment, then Edit, then collect again.
Debt does not exist / entered twiceCancel the record.
Attached to the wrong studentCancel it, then record it again on the correct student.
Management wrote off the debtCancel, and state the reason in Remarks first.

How It Appears on the SOA

Once a back account is Unpaid or Partial, it appears automatically wherever that student's Statement of Account is shown. You do not need to do anything.

On the printed SOA slip

STATEMENT OF ACCOUNT — S.Y. 2026-2027
ABBAS, NADIA M. · Grade 10 — Zainab
Tuition Fee (Month 1)1,200.00
Miscellaneous Fee400.00
School Improvement Fee233.50
Total Amount to be Paid for this month₱1,833.50
⚠ UNPAID BACK ACCOUNT: ₱3,390.00 — S.Y. 2023-2024 (not included in the total above — please settle at the Cashier)
"N O   P E R M I T   N O   E X A M"
Notice what the total says The month's total is ₱1,833.50 — not ₱5,223.50. The ₱3,390 back account is printed as a warning underneath and is deliberately excluded from the amount being billed this month. If a parent asks "why isn't the old balance in my total?", this is the answer: it is billed separately at the Cashier, not through the monthly SOA.

On the Student Portal

The student sees an amber warning panel above their Statement of Account:

⚠️ You have an unpaid back account from a previous school year.
Total outstanding back account: ₱3,390.00 — this is separate from this year's balance below. Please settle it at the Cashier's office.
S.Y. 2023-2024   ₱3,390.00   Unpaid

When does it disappear?

The warning stops showing when…Status becomes
The balance reaches zero — the student paid in fullPaid
The Cashier cancels the recordCancelled

A partial payment does not remove the warning — it reduces the amount shown to the remaining balance.

Rules & Troubleshooting

Rules the system enforces for you

If you try to…The system will…
Collect more than the remaining balanceRefuse, and show the exact balance allowed.
Collect on a fully paid accountRefuse — "already fully paid".
Collect on a cancelled accountRefuse.
Edit the amount below what was already paidRefuse, and name the amount already paid.
Cancel an account that has paymentsHide the Cancel option — void the payments first.
Save without picking a studentRefuse — "select a student first".
Save a zero or negative amountRefuse.
Void the same payment twiceRefuse — "already voided".

Common questions

Question / problemAnswer
"Back-account tables not installed"The database migration has not been run on this server. Contact your I-SMS administrator — they must run migrations/student_back_accounts.sql.
The student search finds nothingType at least 2 characters. Try the surname alone, or the full LRN. Only students with an enrolment record can be selected.
I recorded it on the wrong studentCancel the record and re-record it on the correct student. If you already collected, void the payment first.
The old balance is not on the SOACheck the status — only Unpaid and Partial show. Also confirm the record is on the same student who is enrolled this year.
Should I add the back account to the SOA total?No. The system deliberately keeps them separate. Collect back accounts here, in this module.
Do these payments count in my End-of-Day?Yes — automatically, including voids, which subtract.
Can a student pay in instalments?Yes. Collect any amount up to the balance, as many times as needed. Each payment gets its own OR.
Can I delete a back account?No — use Cancel. Records are never deleted, so the audit trail stays intact.
Everything you do here is logged Creating, editing, cancelling, collecting and voiding all write an audit entry with your name and the time. Work confidently — but know that the record is permanent.
