PART 1 Standard Flow — Generate SOA & Collect Payment
The normal path when a student pays the amount due on their Statement of Account.
1
CashierGenerate the Statement of Account (SOA)
The cashier generates the SOA for a student (or a whole section/grade), choosing the month(s) to bill. The system produces the official SOA slip with a unique, gap-free SOA number and barcode.
cashier › Generate SOA
2
StudentReceives the SOA
The student/parent receives the printed SOA showing the fee breakdown (Tuition, Misc, School Improvement, Books) and the amount due for the month. The barcode lets the cashier pull it up instantly.
◆ Decision: Can the student pay the amount due now?   → YES: continue to Step 3.   → NO: go to Part 2 — Promissory Note (next page).
3
CashierCollect Payment
The cashier scans the SOA / OR barcode (or searches the student), selects the month(s) being paid, enters the amount tendered, and posts the payment.
cashier › Collect Payment
4
SystemPosts payment & issues Official Receipt
In one atomic step the system allocates the payment to the oldest unpaid months, updates the running balance, issues a gap-free Official Receipt (OR), and records it to the ledger and the day's collection.
5
StudentReceives Official Receipt
The student gets the printed OR as proof of payment. Their balance and payment history update immediately — visible in the Student Portal.

↪ End of day & banking

  • The cashier performs an End-of-Day Close (counts cash, records over/short, prints a Z-report).
  • Every Thursday, collected cash is deposited to the bank and a Deposit Certification is printed.
  • A payment posted in error can be Voided (Payment History / Void–Refund), which restores the student's balance.

PART 2 Securing a Promissory Note
Branches from Part 1 when the student cannot settle the current SOA. The note is raised against the current SOA amount, not the whole-year balance.
1
StudentCannot pay — proceeds to the Registrar
Holding the current SOA, the student/parent goes to the Registrar's Office to request a promissory (deferred-payment) arrangement.
2
RegistrarLook up the student & review the SOA
Search by Student ID, LRN, or Name. The system shows the student's current SOA (number, months, amount due), the year balance for context, and any previous promissory notes.
registrar › Promissory Notes › New
3
RegistrarIssue the Promissory Note
Enter the amount covered (up to the current SOA due), the promised payment date, and the reason. The system assigns an ID (e.g. PN-2026-000001) and prints the note — signed by the Registrar and the Student/Parent/Guardian.
4
StudentPresents the note to the Cashier
The student brings the printed Promissory Note to the Cashier's window.
5
CashierVerify the Promissory Note
The cashier scans / enters the PN ID. The system validates it and records it as an approved deferred-payment arrangement (marked “Cashier Verified”).
cashier › Verify Promissory
6
SystemTracks status & flags the SOA
The note stays Pending. The student's SOA shows a warning: “This student has an unpaid promissory note that must be settled.” If the promised date passes unpaid, the note becomes Overdue automatically.
7
StudentPays on/before the promised date
The student returns and pays at the Cashier. Payment is posted normally (Part 1, Steps 3–5) and an OR is issued.
8
SystemNote marked Paid · arrangement closed
The promissory note is set to Paid and the SOA warning clears. If a new SOA is generated while a note is still unpaid, it is flagged so the obligation is never missed.

★ Key rules

  • A promissory note is tied to the current SOA's amount due — the amount covered can never exceed it.
  • Statuses: Pending → Paid (settled) · Overdue (promised date passed) · Cancelled (voided by Registrar).
  • Every Registrar and Cashier action on a note is recorded in the audit log.
  • The Registrar dashboard shows totals: active notes, overdue notes, and the total amount under promissory arrangement.
